# Briefing and Lab B polish

Desktop 1920x1080, Galaxy S23, and Galaxy Tab. Palette stays navy, cyan, and amber. No new art files.

## What changed

- Briefing puts the body scan and a lit manatee on one metal table. A warm key, cool fill, and cyan rim light the scan. The amber line stays on the front lip of the table. `?still=1` and reduced motion hold the lamp, the line, and the turn.
- Lab B keeps the deer and the live oak in the same camera box. The deer stands on a column. The oak keeps a low cap at the roots so the canopy stays in frame. Idle tints separate shell from structure. Bob and the match bridge hold still when motion is off.
- Names live in the panels: Body scan and Manatee on the dispatch card, Deer and Live oak on the match columns. Matched parts show the job word.
- Touch targets are at least 44px, with a pressed state and a hover state only for a fine pointer. Phone briefing shows all four lines and both buttons. Phone Lab B raises the sheet with the dock so the intro, the buttons, and the hint stay in view. On a short landscape phone the cards tighten so the last line and the hint are inside the panel.
