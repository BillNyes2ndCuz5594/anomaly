# Florida Grade 5 Statewide Science Assessment (SSA): assessed NGSSS benchmarks

Compiled 2026-11-01 by a research agent from FDOE documents (the Statewide Science Assessment Test Item
Specifications Version 2 and the Fall 2021 draft, the May 2024 Reporting Category Statements, the Test Design
Summary, the Statewide Assessment Program Information Guide, the content focus reports) and, where an FDOE excerpt
was cut off, Florida district copies of the same specifications (Collier, Polk, Seminole). Status labels: VERIFIED
= quoted from an FDOE document; PARTIAL = part of the text comes from a district copy of the FDOE specs;
UNVERIFIED = not surfaced, do not treat as fact. The teacher should confirm anything marked PARTIAL or UNVERIFIED
against the full PDF at https://www.fldoe.org/core/fileparse.php/5663/urlt/swsatisG5.pdf before relying on it.

Test facts (VERIFIED): the Grade 5 SSA still measures the 2008 NGSSS science benchmarks; 48 to 54 items;
multiple choice only, four options; no calculator; computer adaptive since spring 2024, same blueprint.

## Reporting categories and share of the test (VERIFIED)

| Reporting category | Percent of points |
|---|---|
| Nature of Science | 17% |
| Earth and Space Science | 29% |
| Physical Science | 29% |
| Life Science | 25% |

Grouping rule (VERIFIED): items may be written to "also assesses" benchmarks, but the theme of the grouping must be
evident. Prior-grade knowledge may be required but is not assessed in isolation.

## Item style rules from the specifications (VERIFIED)

- Conceptual items only; no calculator, no scientific notation, no computation.
- Temperature in degrees Celsius with the approximate whole-number Fahrenheit equivalent, e.g. 37°C (99°F).
  Metric units for mass, length, volume.
- The last sentence of every stem is a question; four options; distractors use common misconceptions.
- Grade-level vocabulary (Appendix C glossary); "most likely" and "best" only when the question needs them.
- Words never used or assessed: constellation; waning, waxing, gibbous; radiant; electrons; hypothesis (not
  identified or evaluated); the steps of the scientific method; transpiration, infiltration, percolation; Mohs scale;
  the rock cycle; parallel and series circuits; the climate vs weather distinction; cloud type differences.
- Project rule (from the limits, not a quoted ban): never write "body system", "net force" or "decomposer".
